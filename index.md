Business Problem: Evaluate how traffic congestion impacts economic productivity across major cities worldwide.

Approach: Built an ETL pipeline in Python to clean, integrate, and analyze TomTom Traffic Index and OECD economic datasets.

Key Outcome: Identified relationships between congestion levels, travel delays, GDP per capita, and unemployment to support infrastructure investment decisions.

### 🔹 MercadoLibre Funnel & Retention Analysis

Business Problem: Identify conversion bottlenecks and understand user retention patterns in an e-commerce platform.

Approach: Developed SQL analyses using CTEs, funnel metrics, cohort analysis, and retention calculations (D7, D14, D21, D28).

Key Outcome: Detected funnel drop-off points, measured cohort retention performance, and proposed strategies to improve conversion and user engagement.
